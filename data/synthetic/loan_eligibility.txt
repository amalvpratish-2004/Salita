BUSINESS LOAN ELIGIBILITY POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

IMPORTANT:
This is synthetic/mock business material created for the AI Engineer Assessment.
It does not represent a real financial institution.

1. BUSINESS OPERATING PERIOD

The applicant's business must have been operating for at least 2 years.

Businesses operating for less than 2 years are not eligible for the standard business loan product.

2. MONTHLY REVENUE

The business must have an average monthly revenue of at least INR 50,000.

Revenue may be verified using recent bank statements or other approved financial documents.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Applicants must provide:

- Government-issued identification
- Business registration document
- Recent bank statements
- Proof of business address

4. EXISTING LOANS

An applicant may have existing loans.

However, an applicant with an unresolved default on an existing loan is not eligible for the standard business loan product.

5. LOAN PURPOSE

The loan may be used for legitimate business purposes such as:

- Purchasing equipment
- Working capital
- Business expansion
- Inventory
- Renovation of business premises

6. HUMAN ASSISTANCE

If an applicant has a question that is not covered by this policy, the applicant should be connected with a human representative.

The AI assistant must not invent eligibility rules, interest rates, fees, approval decisions, or other financial commitments.
